## Full description (4000 char max)

```
LibreScan is a free, open-source document scanner for your phone — scan
paper documents, receipts, and notes, clean them up, and export them as
PDFs or searchable text. No account, no cloud, no ads, no subscriptions.

WHY LIBRESCAN

Most "free" scanner apps push a subscription, an account, or ads within the
first few taps. LibreScan doesn't have any of those to push: it's free
software (MIT licensed), the source code is public, and everything it does
happens on your device. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing being
sent anywhere.

FEATURES

• Scan with your camera — point, shoot, done
• Auto-suggested page edges, with full manual control to drag each corner
  into place for a perfectly flat, deskewed page
• Filters: Original, Grayscale, Black & white, and Enhanced (for faded or
  unevenly lit pages)
• Multi-page documents — scan a whole stack, reorder pages, redo any single
  page's crop or filter afterward
• On-device OCR — pull the text out of a page, fully offline
• Export as a PDF, or share just the recognized text
• Save the PDF straight to Downloads, Drive, or any folder you pick

PRIVACY, FOR REAL

LibreScan makes zero network requests. Not "we don't sell your data" —
there is no network code in the app at all, which you can verify yourself
in the source. Every scan lives in the app's own private storage until you
choose to export or share it. Uninstalling the app deletes everything.

OPEN SOURCE

LibreScan is built in the open and licensed under MIT — free to use, modify,
and redistribute. Read the code, report a bug, or contribute:
https://github.com/shubhambansal500/librescan
```
