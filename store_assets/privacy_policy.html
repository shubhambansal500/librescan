LibreScan

Privacy Policy · Last updated October 18, 2026

LibreScan is a free, open-source document scanner. This policy is short for a simple reason: the app doesn't collect, transmit, or store your data anywhere but your own device.

The short version

No accounts. No analytics. No ads. No network requests of any kind. Everything LibreScan does happens on your phone, in your phone's own storage, and stays there until you decide to share or save a file yourself.

01 What LibreScan accesses on your device

  • Camera — to photograph the document you choose to scan. The photo is processed and stored only inside LibreScan's own private app storage.
  • Storage, only when you ask — when you tap Save PDF or Share, LibreScan writes the file to the location you pick (Downloads, a Files app folder, Drive, another app). It cannot read or write anywhere else.

The app never requests microphone, location, contacts, or any other permission. You can check this yourself — see §4.

02 What leaves your device

Nothing — unless you explicitly export or share a file. That action hands the file to whatever destination you chose (your Files app, Drive, email, a messaging app, and so on). From that point on, that destination's own privacy policy applies, not this one.

03 Retention and deletion

Every scan, filter choice, and recognized-text result lives in LibreScan's private, sandboxed app storage on your device. There is no cloud backup, no sync, and no copy kept anywhere else. Uninstalling the app — or clearing its storage from your phone's app settings — deletes all of it, permanently and immediately.

05 Children's privacy

LibreScan does not knowingly or unknowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information from anyone at all.
